# UCLA partner-mark provenance

The homepage uses unmodified, transparent-background department wordmarks from
official UCLA websites. They are kept separate, retain their original aspect
ratios, and use the official light- or dark-background variant with clear
space. CSS switches variants without recoloring the marks.

General department-mark guidance:
<https://brand.ucla.edu/identity/department-logos>

Do not recolor, distort, crop, add effects to, or fuse these marks into a new
logo. UCLA marks may only be used for authorized university business;
third-party uses require permission through <https://marks.ucla.edu/>.
